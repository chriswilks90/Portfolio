Project Overview

A brand new practice needed a
presence that converts.

Proximity Performance is a physical therapy practice launching into a competitive Dallas market. They needed a website that didn't just look professional. It needed to actively work as a lead generation tool, routing visitors toward booking appointments and contacting the practice.

My role was to take the project from zero: no brand, no content structure, no existing digital presence. I led the full UX and web design process including research, wireframing, and final design.

Core Objectives

  • 01 Design an intuitive interface that guides visitors toward scheduling appointments via clear CTAs
  • 02 Establish credibility through competitive analysis and industry best practices
  • 03 Ensure a fully mobile-responsive experience for on-the-go users
  • 04 Maximize lead generation impact on a limited marketing budget

Business Challenges

Five obstacles to
solve from scratch.

01

Brand Identity

No existing brand presence. The site needed to establish trust and professionalism from the first visit.

02

Lead Conversion

Visitors needed to be guided naturally toward scheduling and contact actions, not just informed.

03

Navigation Clarity

Information architecture had to be simple enough that users in pain could find what they need fast.

04

Mobile Responsiveness

Most users would land on the site from a phone. The experience couldn't be an afterthought.

05

Budget Constraints

Maximum marketing impact had to be achieved with limited resources. Every design decision had to earn its place.

User Research

Designing for a
specific someone.

ST

Sarah Thompson

34 · Marketing Manager · Dallas, TX

"I just need to find someone I can trust and book quickly. I don't have time to dig through a confusing website."

Background

Busy professional and mother of two. Recently sustained a back injury while weight training. Looking for a nearby, reputable physical therapist she can trust to help her recover without disrupting her schedule.

Goals

  • Find a highly-rated PT nearby for back recovery
  • Schedule appointments without lengthy phone calls
  • Build confidence through visible credentials and testimonials
  • Track recovery progress between sessions

Pain Points

  • Balancing therapy with work and two young kids
  • Navigating confusing or untrustworthy healthcare websites
  • Long, friction-heavy booking processes

Competitive Analysis

Learning from the
competition.

TideLine Physical Therapy

What they do well

  • Full dedicated services page
  • Prominent reviews and social proof section

Gap identified

No virtual consultation or async booking option

EliteAlliance Dallas PT

What they do well

  • Comprehensive treatment offerings display
  • Clear credential and team pages

Gap identified

Heavy content, slow to reach a CTA for new patients

Our Opportunity

Combine the social proof and services clarity of competitors while adding frictionless booking and a virtual consultation option. This gives new patients a faster, more trusted path to contact.

Task Flows

Two paths to
conversion.

Scenario 1: Phone Consultation

Home → CTA Click → Contact Form → Submit

Scenario 2: Direct Scheduling

Home → CTA Click → Scheduling App → Booked

Design Solutions

Features built to
earn the click.

Virtual Consultation Booking

A free virtual consultation CTA was added as the primary conversion point, lowering the barrier for new patients who aren't ready to commit to an in-person visit immediately.

CRM-Integrated Contact Forms

Contact forms were wired directly to the practice's business email via a CRM/CMS integration, so every lead captured on the site routes immediately to the right person.

Trust-Building Credentials Section

A dedicated section showcasing professional credentials, certifications, and client testimonials. It addresses the trust signals Sarah (and every patient like her) needs before making a decision.

Mobile-First Responsive Design

Given that most local searches happen on mobile, every page was designed mobile-first to ensure the booking CTA is always visible and the experience never breaks on smaller screens.

Outcome

A complete web presence,
built to grow.

Proximity Performance launched with a fully designed website that establishes brand credibility, routes visitors through two clear conversion paths, and integrates directly with the practice's lead management system. The design prioritized what mattered most: getting injured people to the help they need, without friction.
